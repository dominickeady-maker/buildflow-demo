# Request for Construction Site Photo URLs

I need direct URLs to stock photos for a construction management app called BuildFlow. The app is used for residential construction projects and includes photo documentation features.

## What I Need:
Provide 15-20 direct URLs to free stock photos from Pexels that represent typical construction site scenarios. For each photo, include:
1. The direct Pexels URL
2. Brief description of what the photo shows
3. Suggested category (Foundation, Framing, Roofing, Electrical, Plumbing, Safety, Materials, Progress, etc.)

## Photo Types Needed:
- Construction workers wearing safety gear (hard hats, vests, boots)
- Foundation work (concrete, rebar, excavation)
- Framing and structural work (wooden frames, roof trusses)
- Roofing installation
- Electrical and plumbing rough-in work
- Construction materials on site
- Construction equipment and tools
- Site safety examples
- Progress documentation shots
- Close-up detail work
- Wide shots of construction sites

## Format Example:
1. **Foundation - Concrete Pour**
   - URL: https://images.pexels.com/photos/[photo-id]/[filename].jpg
   - Description: Workers pouring concrete for foundation slab
   - Category: Foundation

2. **Framing - Wall Assembly**
   - URL: https://images.pexels.com/photos/[photo-id]/[filename].jpg
   - Description: Wooden wall frames being erected
   - Category: Framing

Please ensure all URLs are direct links to actual Pexels photos that are free to use.
