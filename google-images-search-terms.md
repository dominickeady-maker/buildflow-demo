## Tips:
- Add "royalty free" or "creative commons" to searches if you need copyright-free images
- Use "site:pexels.com" or "site:unsplash.com" for free stock photos
- Look for realistic construction site photos, not stock photos with models
- Save images in JPG or PNG format
- Aim for file sizes under 10MB for faster uploads
